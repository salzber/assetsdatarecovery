🔎 The 5-Second Reality Check

Has someone you've only met online asked you to move money, buy crypto, or pay an "upfront tax" to access funds?

Anatomy of a Crypto Investment Scam ▼
Phase 1

The Hook: Contact initiated via dating app, LinkedIn, or a "wrong number" text.

Phase 2

Trust Building: Weeks of casual conversation, followed by subtle flexes of wealth and a mention of an "uncle" who trades.

Phase 3

The Illusion: You are directed to a highly realistic (but fake) trading platform. Small withdrawals are allowed to build confidence.

Phase 4

The Squeeze: You invest heavy amounts. Suddenly, you must pay "taxes" or "fees" to withdraw. The demands never end.
