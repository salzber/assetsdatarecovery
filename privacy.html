Operational Security

Data Privacy & Security Protocol

Your anonymity and data integrity are our highest priorities. We operate under a strict zero-retention mandate for passive visitors.

01. Core Privacy Directive

OPSEC Level: Maximum

Asset Data Recovery operates fundamentally as an intelligence and advisory platform. We understand that victims of cyber fraud require a secure, sterile environment to assess their options without fear of secondary exploitation. Therefore, our infrastructure is designed to collect absolutely zero personal data from passive visitors.

02. Passive Interaction (Zero-Data Policy)

When you navigate our domain, read our case files, or analyze our threat matrices, you remain completely anonymous. We deploy aggressive anti-tracking measures:

  • ✓ No Identity Scraping: We do not capture IP addresses, geographical coordinates, or device fingerprinting for identification.
  • ✓ No Cookie Exploitation: We do not deploy individualized marketing cookies or cross-site tracking pixels.
  • ✓ No Account Registration: Our intelligence is freely available without requiring an email address or profile creation.

03. Active Triage (Encrypted Communications)

If you choose to initiate a threat assessment via our Secure Chat, you voluntarily enter an active triage protocol. This communication is facilitated via an enterprise-grade secure client (JivoChat).

Data Handled During Triage:

During chat, you may voluntarily disclose transaction hashes, fraudulent URLs, or general case details. We advise you NOT to transmit highly sensitive credentials (like bank account passwords, seed phrases, or full Social Security Numbers) over any chat interface, including ours.

Third-Party Encryption (JivoChat):

Our chat infrastructure is powered by JivoChat. Transmissions are encrypted in transit. By utilizing the chat widget, you consent to their operational privacy policies governing data routing. We do not export your chat logs to external marketing or sales databases. Review their compliance framework here: JivoChat Privacy Policy.

04. Data Non-Distribution Guarantee

Any situational intelligence you provide during a triage session is utilized exclusively to evaluate your exposure and formulate a recovery strategy.

🛑

We Do Not Monetize Victims

Asset Data Recovery will never sell, lease, broker, or trade your contact information, case details, or identity to third-party marketing firms, secondary recovery agencies, or data aggregators.

05. External Jurisdictions

Our operational playbooks frequently mandate that you report incidents to federal entities (e.g., IC3, Action Fraud, Europol) or specific financial institutions. Once you click an external link and navigate away from our domain, you are entering a new digital jurisdiction. Asset Data Recovery is not responsible for the privacy practices, tracking methodologies, or data collection protocols of external government or corporate websites.

Your Anonymity is Protected

You reserve the right to engage our analysts under a pseudonym. If you require clarification regarding this OPSEC protocol, connect via secure chat.
